Proto-drones are [[Silver Forest/Drone|drone]]-like entities but aren’t created or intended as such. These objects are observed to have agency but aren’t entirely intelligent. Though, it’s clear that they can have a mind of their own.

- No proto-drone are alike another.
- Entirely unlike life. Does not require a source to be ‘sustained.’
- Can usually be put back together.

As these are autonomous, there are ways to exploit this for business potential. However, I think we should refrain from further experimentation. Since before [[the incident]], proto-drones were never observed, as far as our records go back. It’s awfully strange that they do suddenly occur now. It’s best that we continue building our own drones ourselves.

-- [[Zolpiden]]

## What they don’t do

- kind control. for now.
- mind control. for now.
- wind control. for now.
