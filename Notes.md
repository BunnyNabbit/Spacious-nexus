An attribute of a [[person]] who takes notes, typically by using software like [[Obsidian]], [[SilverBullet]] or anything of the matter.

It seems noteworthy to figure out if someone does personal knowledge keeping. [[Lies]] won’t be æffective against a database of factual tidbits. People lie all the time, whether intentionally or not, so don’t hold it up to them personally if they do. My policy is to respect the autonomy of all folk.

I’m a bit wary of publishing notes of people. According to most folks on the [[Agora]], usually notes of people with a public presence are published.
