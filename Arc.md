[*Arc*](https://web.archive.org/web/20240127013738/https://arc.io/) (of arc.io) was a peer-to-peer content delivery network. But during its existence, it was more than that.

An interesting aspect of it was that it monetized the bandwidth of users. During 2020-2024, I had a simple goal: to create free web applications while using the least amount of bandwidth. Parked browsers would use their spare bandwidth and storage to power a unique distributed network.

Approval processes were lengthy, and weren’t properly communicated to website owners. The [terms of service](https://web.archive.org/web/20240127013743/https://arc.io/faq/#what-websites-can-use-arc) specified what websites were allowed to use *Arc* or how they could use it. During then, the policy on content deemed “*Facebook* unfriendly” had an æffect on what I did.

[Since its shutdown on 2024](https://web.archive.org/web/20240602022101/http://arc.io/), there hasn’t been anything like it from what I can see. Now, my goal is to just have fun, and leave if it isn’t.
